## Findings

### 20261126-01: Weekly summary email reports streak=0 for every client [P2]

- **File:** `api/cron/weekly-summary.js:86-90`
- **Severity:** P2
- **Category:** correctness
- **Reproduce / failure scenario:** Cron runs Mondays at 14:00 UTC
  (9am ET) per `vercel.json:21`. The streak loop iterates `d = 0..6`,
  starting at `d = 0 = today = Monday`, and `break`s on the first day
  with no log. At 9am Eastern, essentially no client has checked in
  yet for Monday, so `weekData[0][0].result` is null and the loop
  breaks immediately. Every weekly summary email therefore ships with
  `Current Streak: 0 days`, regardless of how consistent the client
  actually is. A client who logged every day of the previous week
  still sees "0 days" in the recap.
- **Why it matters:** The weekly recap goes to every active client
  with `portalEnabled && emailEnabled !== false`. The streak number is
  one of only four stats in the email body (lines 125-134) and sits
  next to "Current Streak:" in large gold type. Showing a zero when
  the client actually has a long streak is demotivating and makes the
  email feel unreliable.
- **Suggested fix:** Count the streak ending Sunday (the last full day
  of the week being summarized), not starting Monday. Shift the loop
  to start at `d = 1` and stop at the first missing day, or compute
  the streak off `client_dailylogs_index:{clientId}` (the ZSET
  already maintained for this client) so it stays consistent with
  what the client sees in the dashboard.
- **Status:** new

### 20261126-02: Engagement email fires every day with no cooldown [P3]

- **File:** `api/cron/engagement-check.js:61-94`
- **Severity:** P3
- **Category:** ux
- **Reproduce / failure scenario:** The daily cron sends a "We miss
  you at Telos" email to any active, portal-enabled client whose last
  daily log is 3+ days old (or absent from the last 5 days). There is
  no record of prior sends, so a client who is away for a week
  receives the same reminder email on day 3, day 4, day 5, day 6,
  and day 7 - five identical "we miss you" messages in a row. A
  client on a two-week vacation would receive ~12.
- **Why it matters:** Looks like spam, encourages unsubscribes from
  the Resend-configured sender, and undermines the credibility of
  future lifecycle emails from the same address.
- **Suggested fix:** Before sending, check / write a per-client key
  such as `client_engagement_email:{clientId}` with a TTL (e.g. 72h
  or 7 days). Skip the send if the key is still set. Alternatively
  store `lastEngagementEmailSentAt` on the client record and gate on
  that.
- **Status:** new

## Memory delta

- Patterns noticed:
  - Several cron / API handlers do "last N days" date math with
    `toISOString().split('T')[0]`, which is UTC. The dashboard runs
    in the user's local tz. This is the server-side cousin of the
    known `todayStr()` client bug.
  - Admin-authored strings are injected into email HTML templates
    without escaping; admin is trusted so this is hygiene, not XSS.
    Noted but not reported.
- False-positive patterns to avoid:
  - Do not re-flag items already in CLAUDE.md's "Known Limitations".
  - The `telosfitnessllc@gmail.com` User-Agent in `api/client/food-search.js`
