# Telos Bug Report - 2026-07-07

**Focus:** Functional
**Run duration:** ~10m
**Findings:** 1

## Summary

One high-confidence P1 in the Execution Score Quiz: the "See Your Score" button is `type="button"` and its click handler never validates the lead-capture form, so users can leave name and email blank and still see their results. On the pricing page this also unlocks pricing without any lead being captured. The Charleston application form and the two tool pages (protein calculator, hyrox predictor) use `type="submit"` and validate correctly, so this is a quiz-specific miss rather than a pattern across the site.

## Findings

### 1. Quiz lead-capture bypass - empty name/email accepted, results and pricing unlocked - [P1]

- **ID:** `2026-07-07-1`
- **File:** `js/quiz.js:225-275` (handler); `index.html:580` and `pricing.html:594` (button markup)
- **Category:** functional
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open `/` (or `/pricing`) and start the Execution Score Quiz.
  2. Answer all 8 questions (any answers work).
  3. On the "Your Score Is Ready" screen, leave the Name and Email inputs blank.
  4. Click "See Your Score".
  5. Observed: results render with the score and pillar breakdown. On `/pricing`, `localStorage.telosQuizCompleted` is set to `"true"` and pricing is revealed. The POST to `/api/submit-quiz` returns 400 ("Name and email required") but the failure is swallowed by `.catch(function(){})` on line 269, and no `.then` inspects the response, so nothing blocks `renderResults()`.
- **Impact:**
  - Every visitor who skips the form still sees results and (on the pricing page) unlocks pricing without giving contact info. Business loses those leads entirely.
  - The `/api/submit-quiz` 400 response is silently discarded because `fetch` only rejects on network errors, not HTTP status; the client never sees the failure and the coach's `/thomas` Leads tab never sees the visitor.
  - Root cause: the button is `<button type="button">` (see `pricing.html:594`, `index.html:580`), and the JS handler in `setupLeadCapture` calls `e.preventDefault()` on a click event. HTML5 `required` attributes are only enforced on real form submission, so with `type="button"` they never fire, and the JS has no independent name/email check.
- **Suggested fix:** Mirror the CHS form pattern (`chs.html:1084-1103`) - either:
  - Change the button to `type="submit"` and bind a `form.addEventListener('submit', ...)` handler that calls `e.preventDefault()` after `form.checkValidity()` gates the flow; or
  - Add an explicit guard at the top of the current click handler: `if (!quiz.userName.trim() || !quiz.userEmail.trim() || !/^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]+$/.test(quiz.userEmail)) { /* show inline error */ return; }` before storing to localStorage or calling `renderResults()`.
  - Also worth having the `fetch` chain read `res.ok` and surface a message on 4xx/5xx so future silent failures are visible.
