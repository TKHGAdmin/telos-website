## BUG-2026-08-05-01 (P1 - Functional)

**Title:** Engagement-check cron sends duplicate "we miss you" emails every day to inactive clients

**File / line:** `api/cron/engagement-check.js:61`

**Summary:**
The `engagement-check` cron runs daily (per `vercel.json`: `0 15 * * *`). It sends a reminder email whenever `lastLogDaysAgo >= 3` (or -1 = no log in 5 days). There is no de-duplication key or cooldown, so an inactive client receives one email every day the cron fires.

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Create an active client with `portalEnabled: true` and `emailEnabled != false`.
2. Have that client stop logging for 3+ days (or never log).
3. Observe daily emails from the cron: day 3, day 4, day 5, day 6, day 7, ...

Concrete outcome: a client inactive for 10 days receives 8 identical "It's been 3+ days since your last check-in" emails (capped-message wording after day 5), one per day. Real user impact: spammy notification pattern, likely spam-flagged by Gmail, damages sender reputation for the `noreply@telosathleticclub.com` from-address (which is also used for weekly summaries and password resets).

**Suggested fix:**
Add a per-client dedupe key with a TTL. Before sending, `SET client_engagement_sent:{id} 1 EX 259200 NX` (3 days). Only send if `NX` succeeded. Clear the key when the client logs in / posts a new daily log.

## BUG-2026-08-05-02 (P2 - Functional)

**Title:** Quiz retake attaches a duplicate submit-lead-capture listener on every retake, causing N duplicate submissions

**File / line:** `js/quiz.js:225` (inside `setupLeadCapture()`)

**Summary:**
`setupLeadCapture()` unconditionally calls `submitBtn.addEventListener('click', ...)`. The function runs once per quiz completion. `resetQuiz()` never removes or replaces listeners, so on the 2nd quiz completion the submit button has 2 listeners, on the 3rd completion 3 listeners, and so on. Each listener fires an independent POST to `/api/submit-quiz`, an independent `localStorage.setItem`, and an independent `renderResults()` call.

The retake button has the same problem: `retakeBtn.addEventListener('click', resetQuiz)` in `renderResults()` (line 397) also accumulates listeners on each render.

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Open `index.html`, take the quiz, click "See Your Score" at lead capture. Observe 1 POST to `/api/submit-quiz` in devtools Network tab.
2. Click "Retake Quiz". Complete the quiz again, click "See Your Score".
3. Observe 2 identical POSTs this time. A third retake produces 3, etc.

Concrete outcome: the admin dashboard `submissions.js` list (ZADD to `quiz_submissions`) fills with duplicate lead entries. On the pricing page overlay flow, the same effect corrupts the `telosQuizCompleted` and `telosQuizData` writes (harmless but wasteful).

**Suggested fix:**
Either (a) attach the submit listener once, outside `setupLeadCapture()`, on `DOMContentLoaded`; (b) replace `submitBtn` with `submitBtn.cloneNode(true)` at the top of `setupLeadCapture()` and re-query it; or (c) track an `_attached` flag on the button.

## BUG-2026-08-05-03 (P3 - Functional)

**Title:** Quiz options remain clickable during 400 ms auto-advance delay, letting a fast tap add extra points to a pillar

**File / line:** `js/quiz.js:198-217` (`handleAnswerSelect`)

**Summary:**
After a user clicks an option, `handleAnswerSelect` adds `option.points` to `quiz.scores[pillar]` and then waits 400 ms before advancing. During that window no button is disabled, `optionsContainer` is not frozen, and clicks on any other option (or a rapid double-tap on the same one) run the handler again — adding more points to the same pillar. Because scores are unbounded per pillar (the pillar breakdown then renders `score / 10 * 100`% width and shows `${score}/10`), the pillar bar can exceed 100% and the total score can exceed the tier ranges (max 40).

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Open `index.html`, start the quiz.
2. On any question, double-tap (or click two different options) within 400 ms.
3. Observe on the final results screen: pillar bar visibly overflows, e.g. Training shows `9/10` and 90% bar even though max legitimate contribution to Training is 10 across its 2 questions — but combined score can now exceed the max tier ceiling of 40.

Concrete outcome: on mobile, an accidental double-tap (very common with iOS Safari's `touchstart` behavior) silently corrupts the score without any user-visible indication. The final tier label (`getResultTier`) may return `undefined` if the total exceeds 40, causing `tier.name` to throw at line 325.

**Suggested fix:**
At the start of `handleAnswerSelect`, disable all option buttons in the container: `optionsContainer.querySelectorAll('.quiz-option').forEach(b => b.disabled = true);` — they get replaced when `renderQuestion()` re-renders on the next question.
