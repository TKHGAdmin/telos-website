Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Telos Athletic Club ("Telos", "we", "us") makes the Telos Method app for iOS. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. Your email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider, never readable by us), the display name you choose, and your year of birth (asked when you sign up, to set your heart-rate zones).

What you log in the app. Workouts and sets (with average and maximum heart rate and time in each heart-rate zone when available), food, weight and body fat, supplement doses, goals, routines, daily tasks, community posts and comments, and messages with your coach.

Health data from Apple Health (only if you connect it). With your permission the app reads: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, all-day heart rate, sleep, steps, active energy, body mass, VO₂ max, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, and workouts. We use this data only to show you your recovery, sleep, load and trends, and to let your coach adjust your training. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties other than the processors below. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Telos.

Lab results (only if you upload them). Blood-panel PDFs or values you enter are stored so you and your coach can review them. An AI service extracts the markers from an uploaded document; the document is not used to train any model.

Body-composition scans (only if you upload them). Photos or PDFs of InBody (or similar) result sheets you add are stored so you and your coach can review them. An AI service reads the values from the sheet; you check them before anything is saved, and the files are not used to train any model.

Heart-rate sensors (only if you connect one). If you pair a Bluetooth heart-rate strap or a watch that broadcasts heart rate, the app reads your heart rate during workouts you start in the app and saves a summary (average and maximum heart rate, time in each zone) with that workout. The app connects to the sensor only while a workout is open.

Device and usage data. Basic diagnostic information (app version, crash reports, device model) to keep the app working. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs and do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

To run the app for you: your Today, Fuel, Move and Core screens; your coach's view of your training, nutrition and recovery; messages; the community feed. To keep the service safe (moderation of reported content). To answer support requests.

Who can see it

  • Your coach at Telos Athletic Club can see the data you log (including lab results and body-composition scans) and, if you connect Apple Health, your recovery, sleep and training-load metrics.
  • Other members can see only what you post in the community and your display name.
  • Service providers that store or process data for us: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), Expo (app builds and diagnostics), Anthropic (AI extraction of uploaded lab documents and body-composition scans). Each acts on our instructions.
  • Strava (optional, only if you connect it): we receive your activities from Strava; we do not write to Strava.

We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

  • Delete your account from inside the app: Profile & settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, logs, goals, health data, lab results, body-composition scans, posts and messages from our systems.
  • Disconnect Apple Health or Strava at any time; data already synced stays until you delete it or your account.
  • Block or report any member or content from the community feed.
  • Email thomas@telosathleticclub.com to request a copy of your data or ask a question.

Retention and security

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes it; backups roll off within 30 days. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers; access on our side is limited to your coach.

Children

The app is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
