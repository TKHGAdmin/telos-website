The science behind the call

HRV + resting HR + sleep ÷ load = today's call.

HRV
112ms

Against their own 14-day normal, with a ±3% band so small wobbles don't change the call.

Resting HR
48bpm

Lower is better. Garmin wearers get a fallback from their lowest overnight readings.

Sleep
96% of need

Need learned from their own nights (7-9 h). Debt adds up over 7 nights.

Load
1.12acute:chronic

Session RPE × minutes, 7-day vs 28-day. 0.8-1.3 is the zone.
